## Source acquisition for legal and interoperability profiles

For each proposed rule or format, retain publisher URL, retrieval time, complete applicable source bytes where permitted, hash, version, effective interval, access limitations, interpretation and reviewer. A portal or search snippet is a discovery lead, not a substitute for a rule/schema bundle. Recheck before activating an implementation; page modification dates are not legal effective dates.

## Activating a company profile

The owner supplies/reviews the profile inventory; the implementer maps every applicable transaction/obligation family to a named supported capability and rule release; the reviewer checks representative and boundary examples; the release artifact proves the implementation; and the operator confirms the required operational/provider arrangement. Only then may the capability be shown as company-verified. If any required row is absent/unknown/unsupported, retain the blocker and offer the bounded supported work that remains available.

A fact change—such as payroll starting, a new currency, a different VAT method, a new framework or a new provider—creates a new effective profile revision and impact analysis. It can stale proposals/readiness without rewriting prior approvals or reports. This is the mechanism for extending the fully planned system without guessing company facts in advance.
