## Financial lock and close protections remain owned

The proposed trigger condition excludes only rows without a recognition voucher. Actual cash-payment recognition seals a voucher using the [retained payment source period and posting date](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-payments.ts:295). The unchanged voucher trigger still rejects a missing retained accounting period or a locked period. Recognized invoice admission still looks up the actual voucher period. A document date must not substitute for either period.

Cash document admission [refuses `StaleDependency` after an executed year-end run covering its document date](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-invoices.ts:267). The [blocking-run query](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/db/commerce/cash-year-end.ts:61) uses `cutoff_on >= documentDate`. Successful admission [bumps the population epoch](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-invoices.ts:421).

Year-end [reads every native invoice through the cutoff](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/db/commerce/cash-year-end.ts:28), including zero-effect members. It rejects unsupported future effects and oversize populations instead of truncating. It [requires an open cutoff period and seals the population epoch](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-year-end.ts:174). Execution [recaptures and compares the full selection](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-year-end.ts:252).

Financial close [requires the complete cash receipt](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/closing/financial-close.ts:386). Its cash owner [compares current membership, retained plan, method witnesses, invoice revision, original basis, and complete recognized-or-credited coverage](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/commerce/cash-year-end.ts:683). The close workflow [rechecks that receipt](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/src/application/closing/financial-close.ts:581). These application checks do not rely on a document-period field.

## Failure and pass expectations before implementation

The following expectations are literal acceptance obligations, not observed results from this review.

1. A qualified retained supplier original with gross `125000`, net `100000`, and VAT `25000` admits a cash invoice. Its recognition is `null`, outstanding is `125000`, and admission adds exactly zero vouchers, journal lines, purchase tax facts, and cash recognition rows. Replay returns the same invoice without another document or population increment.
2. Malformed null recognition on an ordinary invoice, a cash invoice missing its retained draft, or mixed null and nonnull recognition identifiers still fails the existing shape or foreign-key constraint. No register or financial rows commit.
3. A final voucher write whose actual period becomes locked still returns HTTP `409 PeriodLocked`. A final recognized-invoice insert whose recognition voucher period becomes locked still returns HTTP `409 PeriodLocked`. No receipt, invoice, counter, approval consumption, or partial posting commits. Preserve both [existing DF-08 cases](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/tests/period-lock-integrity.e2e.test.ts:23).
4. A commercial cash original dated in a locked interim period may admit when no executed year-end cutoff covers it and all existing source/profile checks pass. Admission still has zero financial effect. Payment or year-end recognition into a locked actual posting period returns `409 PeriodLocked`. This expectation follows the commercial-only contract; it is not a new statutory claim.
5. A new eligible document after year-end preparation makes the sealed execution return `409 StaleDependency`, with financial state and approval use unchanged. Preserve the [existing membership test](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/tests/cash-year-end.e2e.test.ts:494).

Preserve the [existing backdated-discovery test](/Users/admin/.codex/worktrees/whole-year-baseline/openERP/apps/api/tests/cash-year-end.e2e.test.ts:1215).
7. A qualified empty cash year still needs an independently approved complete empty assessment. Financial close cannot bypass it. Changed membership, original basis, reviewed method, or incomplete recognized coverage must block close.
