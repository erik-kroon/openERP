1. Design with retained OpenERP colors, fonts, radii and component constraints.
2. Don't: invent real financial/company facts, hide unknowns behind zeroes, conflate prepared/approved/posted/paid/filed/accepted, add generic KPI walls or chat-first navigation.
3. Use synthetic design data and label its basis.
4. Every frame names its route, job and state. Every meaningful control has a documented destination or state.
5. Keep source, interpreted facts, proposal, authority, accounting result and external outcome distinct.
6. Preserve user work. Paper edits are additions and targeted revisions.
